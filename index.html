Getting Started

Radio: Browse stations by country, genre, or search by name. Click a station to start playing. Heart stations to save them as favorites.

Microphone: Tap the Mic button to visualize live audio from your microphone. Works on mobile and desktop.

Tab Audio: Capture audio from any browser tab (desktop only). Select the tab playing audio when prompted.
